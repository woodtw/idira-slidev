# What Is Idira?

- Next-gen identity security platform, evolved from **CyberArk** in **May 2026**
- Covers **human**, **machine**, and **agentic** identities
- One platform for discovery, privilege control, and governance

# Three Pillars

| Pillar | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Discover Identity Risk** | Find and prioritize identity exposure |
| **Control Every Privilege** | Zero Standing Privileges (ZSP) + Just-in-Time (JIT) access |
| **Automate Governance** | Streamline access reviews and lifecycle |
